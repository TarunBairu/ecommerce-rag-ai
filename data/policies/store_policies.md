
# ShopWise Official Store Policies & Customer Guidelines

Welcome to the ShopWise Customer Policy Guide. All purchases made online at shopwise.com are subject to the terms and guidelines outlined below.

---

## 1. Return and Refund Policy

### 1.1 Standard Return Window

- Customers may return most new, unopened items within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Items must be in their original packaging, including all accessories, manuals, and warranty cards.

### 1.2 Electronics & High-Value Technology

For Consumer Electronics (including laptops, smartphones, headphones, smartwatches, and vacuums):

- **Unopened items:** 30-day return window.
- **Opened items:** Must be returned within **14 calendar days** of delivery.
- Opened electronics are subject to a **10% restocking fee** if missing non-critical packaging or accessories.
- Devices must be factory-reset and unlinked from iCloud, Google Account, or BIOS passwords before return.

### 1.3 Footwear & Apparel

- Shoes and clothing may be returned within **30 days** of delivery.
- Footwear must be unworn and in original condition with shoe box intact.
- Please do not tape shipping labels directly to shoe boxes.

### 1.4 Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned unless received damaged or defective:

- Consumable items (coffee pods, cleaning fluids).
- Gift cards and downloadable software keys.
- Intimate apparel or personalized custom-engraved merchandise.

### 1.5 Return Shipping Fees & Process

- **Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items:** ShopWise provides a prepaid return shipping label free of charge.
- **Buyer Remorse / Change of Mind:** A flat **$6.99 return shipping deduction** applies to the refund when using our prepaid UPS/FedEx drop-off label.
- Alternatively, customers may ship via their own carrier at their expense.
- Refunds are inspected and processed within **3 to 5 business days** of arrival at our fulfillment center.

## 2. Shipping, Delivery & Tracking

### 2.1 Shipping Rates & Tiers (Continental United States)

**Standard Ground Shipping (3–5 Business Days):**

- **FREE** on all orders with a subtotal of **$50.00 and above** (before taxes).
- Flat rate of **$5.99** for orders under $50.00.

**Expedited 2-Day Shipping:**

- Flat rate of **$14.99**.
- Orders placed by **2:00 PM EST** ship the same day.

**Overnight Express (1 Business Day):**

- Flat rate of **$24.99**.

### 2.2 Alaska, Hawaii, and US Territories

- Standard shipping takes 5–8 business days with an additional **$9.99 surcharge**.
- Express options are subject to carrier availability.

### 2.3 International Shipping

- ShopWise currently ships to **Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union**.
- International shipping charges and import duties/taxes are calculated dynamically at checkout based on weight and destination country.
- International returns are accepted; however, customers are responsible for international return shipping and customs clearance.

## 3. Warranty Coverage & Protection Plans

### 3.1 Standard Manufacturer Warranty

- All new hardware, audio equipment, electronics, and small kitchen appliances sold by ShopWise come with a **minimum 12-Month Limited Manufacturer Warranty** unless stated otherwise on the product page.
- Select products have extended warranty periods, such as:
  - **24 months** on select Dyson vacuum cleaners.
  - **18 months** on select Anker audio products.
- Covers factory defects in materials and workmanship.

**The standard manufacturer warranty does NOT cover:**

- Accidental drops.
- Water submersion.
- Normal battery degradation.
- Cosmetic wear.
- Unauthorized third-party repairs.

### 3.2 ShopWise Care Extended Protection Plan

Available for purchase at checkout for all electronics and appliances.

**2-Year Protection Plan:**

- Covers 100% parts and labor.
- Covers accidental spills and drops.
- Covers mechanical breakdowns.
- Covers power surge failures.
- Zero deductible ($0 copay).
- Includes free two-way shipping for repair.
- Provides prompt replacement if the product is unrepairable.

## 4. Price-Match Guarantee

### 4.1 Eligibility Criteria

If you find a lower price on an identical in-stock product from a qualifying national retailer, ShopWise will match the price.

**Eligible retailers:**

- Amazon.com
- BestBuy.com
- Target.com
- Walmart.com

Additional conditions:

- Requests can be made **before purchase**.
- Requests are also accepted within **14 calendar days** of your order delivery date.

### 4.2 Exclusions

Price matching does not apply to:

- Third-party marketplace sellers (e.g., non-Amazon fulfilled sellers).
- eBay listings.
- Clearance products.
- Open-box products.
- Black Friday/Cyber Monday limited doorbuster deals.
- Pricing typographical errors.

## 5. Order Cancellation & Payment Methods

### 5.1 Order Cancellation Window

- Orders enter automated fulfillment rapidly.
- Customers may cancel orders or modify shipping addresses within **60 minutes** of order submission.
- Requests can be submitted directly through the customer account dashboard or by chatting with customer support.
- After 60 minutes, orders cannot be cancelled.
- Customers must follow the standard 30-day return process for eligible products.

### 5.2 Accepted Payment Methods

**Credit & Debit Cards:**

- Visa
- MasterCard
- American Express
- Discover

**Digital Wallets:**

- Apple Pay
- Google Pay
- PayPal

**Installment Financing:**

- Klarna
- Afterpay

Installment financing conditions:

- Split into 4 interest-free bi-weekly payments.
- Available for orders between **$35 and $1,500**.
