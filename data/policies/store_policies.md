# ShopWise Official Store Policies & Customer Guidelines

Welcome to the ShopWise Customer Policy Guide. All purchases made online at shopwise.com are subject to the terms and guidelines outlined below.

---

## 1. Return and Refund Policy

### 1.1 Standard Return Window
- Customers may return most new, unopened items within **30 calendar days** of the delivery date for a full refund to the original payment method.
- Items must be in their original packaging, including all accessories, manuals, and warranty cards.

### 1.2 Electronics & High-Value Technology
- For Consumer Electronics (including laptops, smartphones, headphones, smartwatches, and vacuums):
  - **Unopened items**: 30-day return window.
  - **Opened items**: Must be returned within **14 calendar days** of delivery. Opened electronics are subject to a **10% restocking fee** if missing non-critical packaging or accessories.
  - Devices must be factory-reset and unlinked from iCloud, Google Account, or BIOS passwords before return.

### 1.3 Footwear & Apparel
- Shoes and clothing may be returned within **30 days** of delivery.
- Footwear must be unworn and in original condition with shoe box intact (please do not tape shipping labels directly to shoe boxes).

### 1.4 Non-Returnable Items
- The following items cannot be returned unless received damaged or defective:
  - Consumable items (coffee pods, cleaning fluids).
  - Gift cards and downloadable software keys.
  - Intimate apparel or personalized custom-engraved merchandise.

### 1.5 Return Shipping Fees & Process
- **Defective, Damaged, or Incorrect Items**: ShopWise provides a prepaid prepaid return shipping label free of charge.
- **Buyer Remorse / Change of Mind**: A flat **$6.99 return shipping deduction** applies to the refund when using our prepaid UPS/FedEx drop-off label. Alternatively, customers may ship via their own carrier at their expense.
- Refunds are inspected and processed within **3 to 5 business days** of arrival at our fulfillment center.

---

## 2. Shipping, Delivery & Tracking

### 2.1 Shipping Rates & Tiers (Continental United States)
- **Standard Ground Shipping (3–5 Business Days)**:
  - **FREE** on all orders with subtotal of **$50.00 and above** (before taxes).
  - Flat rate of **$5.99** for orders under $50.00.
- **Expedited 2-Day Shipping**: Flat rate of **$14.99** (orders placed by 2:00 PM EST ship same-day).
- **Overnight Express (1 Business Day)**: Flat rate of **$24.99**.

### 2.2 Alaska, Hawaii, and US Territories
- Standard shipping takes 5–8 business days with an additional $9.99 surcharge. Express options are subject to carrier availability.

### 2.3 International Shipping
- ShopWise currently ships to **Canada, the United Kingdom, and the European Union**.
- International shipping charges and import duties/taxes are calculated dynamically at checkout based on weight and destination country.
- International returns are accepted; however, customers are responsible for international return shipping and customs clearance.

---

## 3. Warranty Coverage & Protection Plans

### 3.1 Standard Manufacturer Warranty
- All new hardware, audio equipment, electronics, and small kitchen appliances sold by ShopWise come with a **minimum 12-Month Limited Manufacturer Warranty** (unless stated otherwise on product page, such as 24-months on select Dyson vacuums or 18-months on Anker audio).
- Covers factory defects in materials and workmanship.
- Does NOT cover accidental drops, water submersion, normal battery degradation, cosmetic wear, or unauthorized third-party repairs.

### 3.2 ShopWise Care Extended Protection Plan
- Available for purchase at checkout for all electronics and appliances:
  - **2-Year Protection Plan**: Covers 100% parts and labor, accidental spills and drops, mechanical breakdowns, and power surge failures with zero deductible ($0 copay).
  - Includes free two-way shipping for repair or prompt replacement if unrepairable.

---

## 4. Price-Match Guarantee

### 4.1 Eligibility Criteria
- If you find a lower price on an identical in-stock product from a qualifying national retailer (**Amazon.com, BestBuy.com, Target.com, Walmart.com**), ShopWise will match the price!
- Requests can be made **before purchase** or within **14 calendar days** of your order delivery date.

### 4.2 Exclusions
- Does not apply to third-party marketplace sellers (e.g., non-Amazon fulfilled sellers, eBay).
- Excludes clearance, open-box, Black Friday/Cyber Monday limited doorbuster deals, or pricing typographical errors.

---

## 5. Order Cancellation & Payment Methods

### 5.1 Order Cancellation Window
- Orders enter automated fulfillment rapidly. You may cancel or modify shipping addresses within **60 minutes** of order submission directly through your account dashboard or by chatting with customer support.
- After 60 minutes, orders cannot be cancelled and must proceed through the standard 30-day return process.

### 5.2 Accepted Payment Methods
- Major Credit & Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express, Discover).
- Digital Wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal).
- Installment Financing: **Klarna and Afterpay** (Split into 4 interest-free bi-weekly payments on orders between $35 and $1,500).
